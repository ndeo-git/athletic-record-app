Admin Roster Packer

Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere. Fill in the roster, press Seal, and paste the output into SEALED_COACHES in the coach app. Keep THIS file and your plaintext roster private; only the sealed output is safe to publish.

Give each coach their passcode privately (in person or a direct message — not in the same channel as the app link). To change anything later — a new student, an email fix, a passcode reset — re-run this packer and replace the block.
